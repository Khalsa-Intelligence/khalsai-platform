Drum Anatomy & Stroke Guide

Foundation Technique Reference
DAYAN (Treble Drum - Right)
SYAHI
• Kinar/Kani (Outer Rim): Sharp ping (Ta / Na)
• Maidan (Middle Leather): Sweet resonant tone (Tin)
• Syahi (Center Circle): Fast muted hits (Tit / Kit)
BAYAN (Bass Drum - Left)
SYAHI
• Open Bass (Ge/Ghe): Strike open surface with Middle/Index pads `[M/I]`.
• Wrist Slide: Modulate bass pitch forward across skin.
• Muted Bass (Ke/Kat): Flat cupped hand slap `[X]`.
