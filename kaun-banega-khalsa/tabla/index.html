Punjabi Bol Guide
Na vs. Ta on the Dayan: Both are crisp rim strikes, but Na rings openly on the kinar/syahi edge while Ta is damped cleanly for a sharp, dry syllable. On the Bayan, Ge opens the bass with finger/wrist resonance and Ke mutes it.
Dayan (Right / Treble Drum)
• Na / Ta: Crisp rim strike on kinar/syahi edge.
• Tin / Tun: Resonant open strike on syahi/maidan.
• Tit / Kit: Fast dry finger strokes on syahi.
Bayan (Left / Bass Drum)
• Ge: Open resonant bass stroke with wrist slide.
• Ke: Crisp damped/muted bass stroke.
• Dha / Dhin: Combined strokes (Na + Ge / Tin + Ge).
