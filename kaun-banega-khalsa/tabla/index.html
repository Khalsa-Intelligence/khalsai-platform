Tabla Bol Masterclass
Khalsai - Tabla App • Acoustic Guide & Stroke Dictionary
The Physics of Na vs. Ta: Both are high-pitched rim strikes on the Dayan (treble drum), but their acoustic roles differ entirely. Na is played openly so the drumhead rings freely (like an open musical vowel). Ta is dampened immediately upon impact by the hand, yielding a dry, sharp "clack" (acting as a crisp musical period during silent wave sections).
Essential Stroke Dictionary
Dha
Compound master stroke: open bass Ge on Bayan + open treble Na on Dayan. The foundational start (Sam).
Dhin / Tin
Resonant middle leather (Maidan) tones on the Dayan played with the index finger. Warm and sustained.
Ge / Ghe
Open bass stroke on the Bayan using flat finger pads with sliding wrist heel modulation.
Tit / Titkit
Fast, crisp double/triple strokes across the black center (Syahi) using middle and ring fingers.
Improvisation: Kayda & Rela
Khalsai - Tabla App • Student Guide to Solo Playing
Beyond playing background rhythmic cycles (Thekas), learning student Kaydas and Relas builds finger dexterity, speed, and structural discipline.
What is a Kayda?
A Kayda ("rule" or "system") is a structured theme composed entirely of primary bols (like Dha, Dhin, Tit, Na). Students expand it using systematic variations called Paltas and finish with a Tihai.
What is a Rela?
A Rela is a rapid-fire, tightly woven composition featuring rolling phrases (like terekete). Played at high velocity, it sounds like a cascading stream of rhythm.
