The Physics of Na vs. Ta: Both are high-pitched rim strikes on the Dayan, but Na is played openly so the drumhead rings freely (open vowel), while Ta is dampened immediately upon impact for a dry, sharp "clack" (crisp period).
DAYAN (Treble Drum - Right)
SYAHI
• Kinar (Outer Rim): Crisp non-resonant pings (Ta / Na)
• Maidan (Middle Leather): Resonant sweet tones (Tin)
• Syahi (Black Center): Fast muted strikes (Titkit)
BAYAN (Bass Drum - Left)
SYAHI
• Open Bass (Ge/Ghe): Strike open surface with finger pads. Allow bass to ring.
• Wrist Modulation: Slide wrist forward across skin to bend pitch.
• Muted Bass (Ke/Kat): Flat cupped hand slap.

Improvisation: Kayda & Rela
Khalsai - Tabla App • Student Guide to Solo Playing
Beyond playing background rhythmic cycles (Thekas), learning student Kaydas and Relas builds finger dexterity, speed, and structural discipline.
What is a Kayda?
A Kayda ("rule" or "system") is a structured theme composed entirely of primary bols (like Dha, Dhin, Tit, Na). Students expand it using systematic variations called Paltas and finish with a Tihai.
What is a Rela?
A Rela is a rapid-fire, tightly woven composition featuring rolling phrases (like terekete). Played at high velocity, it sounds like a cascading stream of rhythm.
